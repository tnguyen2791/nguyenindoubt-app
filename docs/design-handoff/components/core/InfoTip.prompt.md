Education affordance: a small italic-i dot next to any marker name; tap opens a plain-language card. Place one beside every metric a newcomer might not know (HRV, readiness, temperature deviation, SpO₂).

```jsx
Resting HR <InfoTip term="Resting heart rate">Your heart's pace at complete rest.
A lower morning value than usual often just means good recovery.</InfoTip>
```

Voice rules: 1–2 sentences, plain words, end on reassurance or a next step — never a warning. The tooltip explains what the marker *is* and what a change *usually* means.
