NguyenInDoubt
Welcome · getting started

When in doubt, check in — don't freak out. NguyenInDoubt reads your body's quiet signals — sleep, heart rate, temperature — and tells you what they mean in plain words. Every reading compares you to your own typical range, never to anyone else. No streaks, no guilt, no alarms.

Getting started
1
Wear the ring

Snug on your index or middle finger, sensor on the palm side. Charge it every few days — it'll tell you when.

2
Let it learn you

The first two weeks are calibration. Missed days are fine — they just extend it a little.

3
One reading a morning

That's the whole habit. Open the app, read the note, get on with your day.

Sharing — entirely optional

You choose what's seen, if anything

If you'd like to share your readings, open Profile → Request to share and find your provider. You pick exactly what's shared — summaries, sleep detail, journal tags — and your written notes always stay yours.

Sharing is read-only, you see everything they see, and you can pause it anytime. No notice goes out when you do.

How it works

1. You opt in and choose what to share
2. Your provider accepts the request
3. Pause or change it anytime — no notice goes out
Learn as you go
  • Tap any ⓘ dot to get a plain-language explanation of a marker — HRV, readiness, temperature drift.
  • The Explore tab has short reads and practices — stress, sleep, and low days included.
  • Box breathing (4 minutes) is a good first practice for a racing evening.
Good to know
  • Ember-colored flags mean "worth watching," never "panic." One odd reading is noise; three is a pattern.
  • Tags explain your scores — they never change them.
  • Your data stays yours. Processed on your device, never sold, never shared with advertisers.

NguyenInDoubt is educational — it never diagnoses, and it's not a substitute for care.
