NguyenInDoubt
Brand one-pager · Jul 2026

When in doubt, check in — don't freak out. NguyenInDoubt is a recovery and sleep companion that reads your body's quiet signals and tells you what they mean, not what to fear. The name owns the uncertainty: scores compare you to your own baseline — never to anyone else — and the product says "worth watching, not worrying" instead of sounding alarms.

Numbers are the heroes: one big readout per metric with a small unit and a short qualitative caption.

Data-display principles

One accent per metric. Muted, low-saturation base; a single green carries the metric, ember marks only off-target states. Color is meaning, never decoration.

Rings & rounded bars. 0–100 summaries as soft rings; trends as rounded bars, value labeled above, minimal gridlines.

Generous whitespace. Roomy padding, large radii, soft edges. Let the number breathe.

Gentle motion. Values ease in; nothing pops. Transitions feel restful — this is a sleep product.
