<objective>
Make the clinician dashboard's invite-status rows tell the truth about tappability (SAFE-02). Today every row is wrapped in the same `InkWell` and `SectionCard`; only accepted rows have a non-null `onTap`, so pending/revoked/expired rows still present a tappable-looking card with ripple that silently absorbs a tap. Reserve the tappable card + chevron for rows that actually respond, and render non-actionable rows as visibly disabled (reduced opacity, no ink ripple, no chevron), letting the status pill carry the "why".

Purpose: A card that looks tappable must be; anything inert must not present hover/ripple affordances. This removes the affordance lie on the clinician surface without changing any privacy behavior.
Output: A reworked `_PatientList` row builder and a focused widget test proving the actionable/inert distinction.
</objective>
