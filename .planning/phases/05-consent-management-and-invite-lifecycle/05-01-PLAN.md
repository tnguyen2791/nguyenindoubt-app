## Must Haves

### truths
- Patient validation of a pending invite shows a preview without granting access.
- Invalid, expired, revoked, missing, malformed, and wrong-patient invite codes grant no access.
- Acceptance writes consent status, link status, timestamps, and one consent history event.
- Revocation immediately removes clinician sleep-summary access.
- Clinician status rows expose lifecycle state but only accepted rows expose sleep summaries.
- Consent history persists locally and contains metadata only.
- Firestore rules still deny clinician journal reads for every link state.

### prohibitions
- statement: MUST NOT grant clinician sleep access from validation alone.
  status: resolved
  verification: test
- statement: MUST NOT expose journal entries, drafts, or private reflections through invite, status, history, or clinician surfaces.
  status: resolved
  verification: test
- statement: MUST NOT let a clinician self-create or self-accept a link through client-side writes.
  status: resolved
  verification: test
- statement: MUST NOT use coercive copy that pressures the patient to share data or frames revocation as unsafe/noncompliant.
  status: resolved
  verification: judgment
- statement: MUST NOT store raw journal text or raw sleep samples in consent history.
  status: resolved
  verification: test
