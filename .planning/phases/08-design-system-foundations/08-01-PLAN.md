<objective>
Establish the design-system foundation the rest of v1.1 consumes: a real typography ladder, spacing/radius tokens, a state-driven StatusPill, a neutralized Material color scheme, and one BrandMark widget. This plan edits only the theme and shared primitives — screens are swept in Wave 2 (08-02, 08-03).

Purpose: Every later surface (onboarding, data-viz, sharing, motion) must inherit deliberate tokens instead of templated Material. This is the horizontal base; it changes look, never behavior or copy.
Output: `lib/theme/tokens.dart` (new), a rebuilt `lib/theme/app_theme.dart` theme, and tokenized/de-weighted primitives plus new `PillTone` + `BrandMark` in `lib/screens/common_widgets.dart`.

Implements DS-01 (type ladder), DS-02 (space/radius tokens), DS-03 (PillTone + StatusPill), DS-04 (seed-leak neutralization), DS-05 (BrandMark widget).
